RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 1 January 2026 between Ravi Kumar ("Landlord") and Anita Sharma ("Tenant"), for the property at 12 MG Road, Kurnool.

1. TERM
The lease commences on 1 February 2026 and continues for 11 months. The lease shall automatically renew for further 11-month terms unless either party gives 30 days written notice.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 15,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. A security deposit of Rs. 45,000 is payable, which is refundable within 30 days of vacating. Late payments attract interest of 2% per month.

3. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving 30 days written notice.

4. LIABILITY
The Landlord shall not be liable for any indirect or consequential damages.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India and courts in Kurnool shall have jurisdiction.
