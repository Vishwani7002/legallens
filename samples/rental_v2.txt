RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 1 January 2026 between Ravi Kumar ("Landlord") and Anita Sharma ("Tenant"), for the property at 12 MG Road, Kurnool.

1. TERM
The lease commences on 1 February 2026 and continues for 11 months. The lease shall automatically renew for further 11-month terms unless the Tenant gives 90 days written notice.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. A non-refundable security deposit of Rs. 90,000 is payable. Late payments attract interest of 5% per month.

3. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time, in its sole discretion, and may evict the Tenant without prior notice.

4. LIABILITY
The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against any and all claims, losses and expenses.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India and courts in Kurnool shall have jurisdiction.
